Municipality Reports

Search for a municipality below and open its report. Municipality reports summarize all commercial and industrial PINs located in that municipality, including municipalities that cross township boundaries.

Because projects may cross municipal or township boundaries, report counts are not additive across geographies.

Total counts cover 2006–2024. The 2024 columns count PINs observed that year and projects with at least one of those PINs, using the reviewed project assignments.
