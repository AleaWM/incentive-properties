What types of property are these PINs in 2024?

This table counts individual PINs by their 2024 assessor property classification, with the class description beside each code. It does not count projects: one project can contain PINs in several classes. Because the sample includes properties that were commercial or industrial in earlier years, residential, vacant, and exempt classes can also appear. The final missing-record category has no known 2024 classification.

Projects in Willow Springs

Counts and values include only PINs within this region. A project may extend into another region, so regional project counts are not additive. Unassigned PINs are excluded from project totals. FMV means estimated fair market value. Incentive PINs are 2024 classes 600-899. FMV is summed only over known 2024 values; an entirely missing valuation remains missing. The project identifier uses Appellant, falling back to Key PIN. Last Year of Paid Taxbill lists each distinct latest year in which a PIN had a bill greater than $0 during 2006-2024; it does not confirm payment. PINs without a positive bill in that history are identified separately. TIF counts use 2024 PIN-to-TIF membership records regardless of payment or increment, rather than positive distributions. Missing 2024 records are not counted as known TIF members.

Sources and coverage

Historical universe: commercial_industrial_pin_universe_2026_09_21.csv, derived from comm_ind_PINs_ever_2006to2024.csv. Classification and FMV: 2024 observations in that underlying extract. Project assignments: project_assignments_phase7_2026_09_22.csv (phase-7 key PIN). Appellants and appeal years: latest tax-year records for each PIN in borappeals_UPDATED.csv, including all ties. Names come only from BOR records for that PIN, with no fallback to reviewed names. Buyer names come from the assignment file; purchase years are matched by PIN, sale document, and buyer in Assessor_Parcel_Sales_20250105.csv. Unmatched years are shown as unavailable. Last positive bills use the 2006-2024 history, and TIF membership uses tif_record_present in the 2024 extract. Class descriptions: class_dict_expanded.csv. Geometry: 2024 footprints from ptaxsim-2024.0.0.db.

PIN totals come from the tabular universe, independently of map coverage. Footprints are drawn once even when multiple PINs share the same ten-digit identifier. Assignment-review counts identify flagged records; absence of a flag does not certify an assignment. Directory and PIN-search pages may still use older data vintages.
